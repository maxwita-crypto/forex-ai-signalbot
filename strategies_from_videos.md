# Video research workflow (not auto-learning from YouTube)

The app does NOT automatically watch thousands of videos. It cannot verify whether a video strategy is profitable just because it sounds convincing. To extend it safely:

1. Obtain video transcripts with permission and reliable timestamps/metadata.
2. Extract candidate rules into structured specifications (entries, exits, sessions, spreads, no-lookahead).
3. Reject vague/non-testable claims; do not deploy scraped strategies automatically.
4. Independently backtest on tick/bar data with actual costs, then out-of-sample and paper trade.
5. Promote only after quantitative review. Keep a provenance log and rollback option.

Potential future services: a scheduled transcription/research pipeline; a licensed economic calendar data provider; real broker market data for outcome attribution.
